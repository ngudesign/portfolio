About

Hi, I'm Lisa.Trúc.

A product designer based in City, currently shaping tools that help teams do their best work.

Background

I've spent the last eight years designing digital products across fintech, healthcare, and productivity software. I started in visual design, moved into interaction design, and now lead end-to-end product work — from framing the problem to sweating the final details.

I care about clarity over cleverness. My best work comes from close collaboration with engineers and product partners, a habit of testing early, and a willingness to throw away the first idea.

Outside of work, you'll find me reading, cooking for friends, or wandering a new city with a film camera.
